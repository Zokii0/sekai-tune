Frequently Asked Questions

What is Sekai Tune?

Sekai Tune is a high-performance, open-source YouTube Music client for Android. It features a modern Material 3 design and is built specifically for users who prioritize privacy and an enhanced listening experience.

Is Sekai Tune completely free and ad-free?

Yes. Because it is an open-source project, Sekai Tune is completely free to use. There are no paid tiers, no mandatory subscriptions, and no audio or visual ads inside the app to interrupt your music.

Can I download songs to listen offline?

Yes, Sekai Tune fully supports local offline playback. You can download your favorite tracks directly to your Android device and listen to them anytime without an active internet connection.

Does the app display synced lyrics?

Yes, Sekai Tune includes built-in support for real-time synced lyrics, allowing you to follow along with the words as the music plays.

Is Sekai Tune available on iPhone (iOS) or Windows PC?

Currently, Sekai Tune is exclusively developed as an Android application.

Do I need a YouTube Premium account to use it?

No, a premium subscription is not required. You can enjoy advanced features like background playback, an ad-free experience, and local playback entirely for free through the app.
